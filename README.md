## General info
- My project, Bridging Cultures Through Music, is a code-based musical composition created in TunePad that blends traditional Vietnamese music with modern pop to explore how technology and creativity can promote cultural representation and identity in global music.
- The project addresses the underrepresentation of non-Western music in mainstream spaces by challenging how music algorithms prioritise Western sounds and limit exposure to diverse cultural traditions.
- The purpose of my project is to celebrate and reconnect with my Vietnamese heritage while broadening the mainstream music landscape through a fusion of Vietnamese and pop elements that promote cultural appreciation and inclusivity.
- I undertook this project to bridge my personal disconnection from Vietnamese culture growing up in Australia and to creatively challenge cultural bias while developing new coding and music composition skills.
